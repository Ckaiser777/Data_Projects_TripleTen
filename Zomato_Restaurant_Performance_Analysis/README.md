
# Zomato Restaurant Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes restaurant performance using Zomato data to understand how cuisine type, location, pricing, and customer ratings influence overall success. The goal was to identify high‑performing restaurants, uncover patterns in customer preferences, and highlight operational opportunities for improvement. The final Tableau dashboard provides an interactive view of cuisine trends, rating distributions, and geographic performance across key markets.

---

## Project Structure
- **Data Cleaning:** Standardized cuisine labels, normalized rating fields, handled missing values, and created helper fields for analysis.
- **Feature Engineering:** Added cuisine groupings, price buckets, and rating categories to support deeper segmentation.
- **Exploratory Analysis:**
  - Cuisine popularity and demand patterns  
  - Rating distribution across cities and price tiers  
  - Restaurant density and geographic performance  
  - High‑performing restaurants by rating and review volume  
- **Dashboard Development:** Built an interactive Tableau dashboard to visualize cuisine trends, restaurant performance, and customer rating behavior.
- **Executive Summary:** Synthesized insights into clear recommendations for restaurant operators and food‑tech stakeholders.

---

## Key Insights
- North Indian, Chinese, and Fast Food cuisines dominate availability and customer demand across major cities.
- Higher‑priced restaurants tend to receive better ratings, but mid‑priced restaurants generate the highest review volume.
- Cities with dense restaurant clusters show more competition but also higher customer engagement.
- Restaurants with diverse cuisine offerings tend to attract more reviews but do not always achieve higher ratings.
- The strongest performers combine high ratings with consistent review volume, indicating both quality and customer trust.

---

## Tools Used
- Tableau (interactive dashboard, geographic analysis, cuisine segmentation)
- Excel (data cleaning, feature engineering, exploratory analysis)
